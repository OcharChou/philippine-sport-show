About the Cluster

The Pearl River Delta hosts a large concentration of sports surface, court material, venue facility and engineering service companies. Because these companies are spread across Guangzhou, Dongguan, Foshan and neighbouring cities, the supply base is best presented as an integrated sports flooring and facility supply chain rather than a single-county cluster.

Buyers benefit from one-stop delivery: material supply, court equipment, installation design and construction support can be sourced together, which reduces coordination risk on school, community and stadium projects.

Core Product Categories

Product ranges available from OEM and ODM manufacturers within this cluster.

Silicone PU Court Systems

Elastic acrylic and silicone PU systems for basketball and multi-sport courts.

Acrylic Coatings

Hard court acrylic coatings for tennis, pickleball and basketball courts.

EPDM & Running Tracks

EPDM granules, rubber tracks and athletics surfacing systems.

PU Flooring

PU sports flooring for indoor halls and multi-purpose venues.

PVC Sports Flooring

PVC and vinyl sports floors for indoor courts and gyms.

Rubber & Modular Flooring

Rubber rolls and interlocking modular tiles for courts and gyms.

Wooden Sports Flooring

Maple and engineered wood systems for indoor competition halls.

Artificial Turf

Football, landscape and multi-sport turf with infill options.

Fencing, Lighting & Seating

Perimeter fencing, mast lighting and spectator seating systems.

Hoops, Nets & Scoreboards

Basketball hoops, nets, posts and electronic scoreboards.
