About the Cluster

Jinjiang has one of the most complete sports footwear and apparel supply chains in China, spanning textile materials, shoe materials and soles, footwear and garment machinery, finished shoes, sports apparel and brand operations.

The city hosts international sports brands as well as a very large base of OEM/ODM suppliers and material companies, which allows buyers to source finished products, develop private labels, or purchase materials and components directly.
