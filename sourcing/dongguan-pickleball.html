About the Cluster

Dongguan is one of the world's most important manufacturing cities. As the global pickleball market expands, existing racket, composite material, tooling, plastics and packaging companies have moved quickly into pickleball equipment, creating a strongly export-oriented supply cluster.

Clear agglomeration has appeared in areas such as Qingxi, where suppliers can deliver a complete OEM/ODM service from raw material and core development through to finished paddles, balls, nets, kits and retail packaging.
