About the Cluster

Yongkang has grown its sports goods industry on top of a very strong hardware, metal processing and machinery manufacturing base. The result is a diversified product system covering fitness equipment, scooters, leisure and outdoor products, and rehabilitation equipment.

The cluster's core strength is the concentration of metalworking, tooling, plastics, motors and surface treatment processes within one city, which shortens development cycles and keeps OEM/ODM programmes cost-competitive.
