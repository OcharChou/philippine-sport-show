About the Cluster

Xindian Town in Rudong County has a long history of fitness equipment manufacturing and has formed a cluster built around dumbbells, barbells, weight plates, training benches and home fitness products.

A large number of local companies have worked on OEM and export programmes for many years. Products are highly standardised and available across wide SKU ranges, which makes the cluster especially efficient for retail chains, e-commerce sellers and multi-category importers.

Core Product Categories

Product ranges available from OEM and ODM manufacturers within this cluster.

Barbells
