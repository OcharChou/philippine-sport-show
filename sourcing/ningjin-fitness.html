About the Cluster

Ningjin in Dezhou is one of China's most representative fitness equipment manufacturing clusters, with particular strength in commercial strength training equipment. The county has built a highly integrated supply chain covering steel processing, laser cutting, tube bending, welding, coating, upholstery, weight stacks, electronic control systems, final assembly and export.

The cluster is geared towards gym operators, hotel and property projects, institutional buyers and international distributors that need full commercial gym fit-outs rather than single consumer products.
