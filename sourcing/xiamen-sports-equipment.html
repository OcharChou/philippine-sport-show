About the Cluster

Xiamen is one of China's important sports equipment manufacturing and export cities. It has built a diversified industrial system covering fitness equipment, sports rehabilitation devices, carbon fibre composite sporting goods, and outdoor and water-sports products.

The city's sports manufacturing is tightly integrated with electronics and information technology, metal processing, composite materials, the health industry and international trade. The result is strong OEM/ODM capability, intelligent product development and mature export services supported by port logistics and cross-border e-commerce.
