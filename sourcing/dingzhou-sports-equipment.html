About the Cluster

After many years of development, Dingzhou has built a comprehensive product system covering school sports, public fitness, athletics, ball sports, martial arts and children's sports facilities, making it one of China's more representative general sports equipment manufacturing clusters.

The cluster is particularly strong at project-based supply: manufacturers can bundle equipment, installation guidance and spare parts into packages for schools, government programmes, community parks and venue contractors.
