About the Cluster

Shangguan in Fuyang has decades of racket manufacturing history and is one of China's most representative racket industry clusters. Local production has evolved from bamboo and metal rackets to carbon fibre, composite and high-performance models, supported by a complete chain covering raw materials, moulds, frame making, forming, painting, printing, stringing, handles and packaging.

Fuyang is now promoting coordinated development across Shangguan, Huanshan, Longmen and Changlv to further strengthen cluster density and shared services for export-oriented manufacturers.
