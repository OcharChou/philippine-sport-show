About the Cluster

Yongning in Shishi has a long-standing base in sports goods and racket manufacturing. The local area continues to encourage racket company agglomeration and industrial park development, drawing on Quanzhou's mature footwear, apparel, sports goods, foreign trade and logistics systems.

Product development focuses on badminton rackets, tennis rackets and pickleball paddles, supported by composite material processing, frame production, surface treatment and assembly capabilities concentrated within a compact industrial area.

Core Product Categories

Product ranges available from OEM and ODM manufacturers within this cluster.

Badminton Rackets

Recreational, intermediate and competition frames for retail and club channels.

Tennis Rackets

Adult and junior frames with OEM graphic and specification options.

Pickleball Paddles

Entry-level to mid-range paddles for brands and distributors.

Carbon Fibre Rackets

Composite frames using local carbon material and moulding capacity.

Racket Frames

Bare frames and semi-finished frames for brand assembly programmes.

Racket Bags

Branded bags and covers produced alongside racket programmes.

Racket Accessories

Strings, grips, dampeners and spare components.
