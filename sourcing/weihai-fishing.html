About the Cluster

Weihai is one of the world's most important fishing tackle manufacturing regions, with a complete industrial system running from raw materials and production equipment through components to finished rods, reels, lines and lures.

Building on a strong carbon fibre industry base, the cluster is also extending into higher-performance outdoor and water-sports products. Own-brand manufacturing and OEM programmes run side by side, giving buyers both speed and flexibility.

Core Product Categories

Product ranges available from OEM and ODM manufacturers within this cluster.

Fishing Rods

Casting, spinning, fly and saltwater rods in carbon and composite builds.

Fishing Reels

Spinning, baitcasting and conventional reels with OEM options.

Fishing Lines

Monofilament, fluorocarbon and braided lines across all strengths.

Hooks & Terminal Tackle

Hooks, swivels, sinkers, snaps and rig components.

Artificial Lures

Hard baits, soft baits, jigs and metal lures with custom finishes.

Guides & Components

Rod guides, reel seats, handles and rod-building components.

Tackle Bags & Storage

Tackle bags, boxes, backpacks and waterproof storage.

SUP & Kayak Accessories

Paddle boards, paddles, kayak fittings and water-sports accessories.

Outdoor Water-Sports Gear

Products extending into broader outdoor and marine leisure use.
