About the Cluster

Xiaoji Town in Jiangdu District is one of China's more representative production areas for sports protective gear, with manufacturing capability in knee, wrist, waist and other support products as well as small fitness accessories.

The cluster is closely tied to e-commerce, OEM customisation and export sales. Compact product size, a very wide SKU range and flexible customisation make it efficient for online retail and multi-category importers.

Core Product Categories

Product ranges available from OEM and ODM manufacturers within this cluster.

Knee Supports

Sleeves, straps and hinged supports for running, gym and court sports.

Wrist Supports

Wrist wraps and supports for lifting, racket sports and gymnastics.

Elbow Supports

Compression sleeves and straps for training and racket sports.

Ankle Supports

Ankle braces and sleeves for running, basketball and football.

Waist Supports

Waist trimmers and lumbar supports for training and daily use.

Weightlifting Belts

Leather and nylon lifting belts with custom branding.

Sports Supports & Braces

General support products across a wide size and colour range.

Training Gloves

Gym gloves, grips and hand protection products.

Resistance Bands & Tubes

Latex bands, tubes and portable resistance training kits.

Small Fitness Accessories

Straps, wraps, sleeves and other add-on fitness items.
