About the Cluster

Hebi is one of the Chinese cities where pickleball has grown fastest in recent years. Using its functional new materials, rubber and plastics manufacturing base, the city is developing paddles, balls and related production equipment while simultaneously building courts, hosting tournaments and stimulating local sports consumption.

The result is a manufacturing-plus-market ecosystem: equipment production, event operations, venue services and training programmes reinforce each other, which is particularly attractive for new brands, entry-level sets and tournament equipment packages.
