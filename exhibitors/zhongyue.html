Sports Facilities

Sports facility supplier specializing in acrylic PVC flooring and court-surface systems for pickleball and other indoor and outdoor sports venues.

About the Supplier

Zhongyue Sports Industry (Guangdong) Co., Ltd. supplies sports-facility systems and court-surface solutions. Its ZOYAK Peak Ball Flooring Series includes acrylic PVC sports flooring designed for pickleball venues, with 3.0 mm products for indoor and outdoor use. Product materials highlight scratch resistance, UV protection, wear resistance, waterproof performance, flexibility, color consistency and resistance to delamination. Public ITF records also identify Zhongyue Sports Industry (Guangdong) Co., Ltd. as the client for an ITF One Star recognised acrylic tennis-court installation at Dashahe International Tennis Exchange Center in Shenzhen.
