About the Supplier

Founded on June 21, 2017, Zhongshan Yuqiu Lighting focuses on industrial lighting and sports lighting. The company has a dedicated sports lighting subsidiary and a construction company with construction qualifications. Equipped with professional optical engineers and testing equipment. Holds 10+ design patents and 1 national invention patent. Served major projects including FAW Toyota, Wuhan Steel, CRRC, and Vipshop warehouse. Brands include Aihuafili, Pingfan Shiguang, and Mavos.
