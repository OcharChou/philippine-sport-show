About the Supplier

Guangzhou Henghui Sports Facilities Co., Ltd., founded in 2007, develops, manufactures and supplies sports-surface materials and provides solution planning, material supply and construction support. Its verified product range includes plastic running tracks, silicon PU courts, acrylic courts, polyurethane PU courts, EPDM flooring, safety mats, suspended flooring, artificial turf and playground equipment.
