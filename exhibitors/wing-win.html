About the Supplier

Our company, established in 2004, has over 20 years of experience in producing underwear for men, women, and children. We operate factories in Guangxi (with over 600 employees and 14 production lines) and Myanmar. Our products have obtained multiple certifications, including BSCI and Oeko-Tex Standard 100. We have long-term cooperation with many international brand customers, including AVON Philippines and Jockey Philippines.
