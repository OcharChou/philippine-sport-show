About the Supplier

IANONI is a racket-sports brand with a strong focus on pickleball. Its Philippine product range includes carbon-fiber and recreational pickleball paddles, pickleballs, portable net systems, paddle sets and sports bags. The IANONI brand was developed in the USA and its products are manufactured by Caston Sports Manufacturer in China; the brand has promoted pickleball through its 'IANONI PICK A BALL' initiative since 2017.
