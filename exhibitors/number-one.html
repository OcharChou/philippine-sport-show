About the Supplier

NUMBER ONE is a subsidiary of IBMC Group, which was established in 2009 and is headquartered in Quanzhou, Fujian. The group owns 9 subsidiaries and has offices in the Philippines, Hong Kong, Brazil, and other locations. The main products of the group include footwear, apparel, bags and accessories, with operations covering product design, R&D, manufacturing, global supply chain integration, cross-border e-commerce, new media operations and cross-border e-commerce services. IBMC Group is a multi-industry multinational group with a platform-based model. The company has over 1,000 employees and has production bases for footwear, apparel and bags in Fujian. It operates 15 international footwear and apparel manufacturing lines, producing over 2.5 million pairs of shoes annually, and more than 30 million units of apparel, bags and accessories. The annual turnover exceeds $80 millions.
