About the Supplier

Infinity Sports International, Inc. has operated in the Philippines since 2003 as an importer and distributor of sporting goods as well as school and office supplies. The company serves department stores, sports shops, schools, wholesalers and retailers nationwide and distributes established international brands. Its sports portfolio includes equipment for basketball, volleyball, soccer, badminton and pickleball, among other sporting goods.
