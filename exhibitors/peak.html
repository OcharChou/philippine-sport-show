About the Supplier

Founded in 1994 (brand since 1989), PEAK is a leading Chinese sports brand integrating R&D, production and sales of sports shoes, apparel, and bags. With 1,000-3,000 employees and 6,000+ global retail outlets, the company was formerly listed on the HKEX. Products are exported to Europe, North/South America, Africa, Middle East, and Oceania. One of China's top three basketball shoe brands.
