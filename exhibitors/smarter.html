About the Supplier

Fujian Smarter has been providing OEM/ODM services for more than 25 years. Equipped with 10 rainwear/garments production lines and fabric manufacturing workshops, our products range is various of rainwear, protective clothing, outdoor wear etc.. Most of our products are delivered to European American and Asian markets. We have been ISO9001:2015 certified since June, 2002, we also have BSCI and SEDEX certificate. Our product conforms the standards of USA and EU, such as EN ISO14116-2008, ASTM D6413-2008, CA PRO65 ANSI/ISEA 107-2010 etc..
