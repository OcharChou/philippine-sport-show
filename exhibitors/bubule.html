About the Supplier

Zhejiang Bubule Bags & Cases Co., Ltd. was founded in 1997 and manufactures PP/PC trolley luggage and hard-shell cases. Public supplier information verifies manufacturing, OEM/design services and ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and OHSAS 18001 certifications. Its sports relevance is primarily in team travel, athlete travel and event logistics rather than specialist sporting equipment.
