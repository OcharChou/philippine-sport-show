About the Supplier

PSI Sports Inc. is a Philippine wholesale trading company established in 2010. It offers more than 1,000 sports products across basketball, volleyball, soccer, tennis, table tennis, badminton, pickleball, fitness, boxing, camping, track & field and other categories. The company imports and distributes multiple sports brands and also supplies school and office products.
