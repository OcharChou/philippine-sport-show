About the Supplier

Founded on June 13, 2016, HUGE SPORTS & ATHLETICS is part of the Hongs group (established 1988 in Hong Kong). The group integrates 800 staff and serves retailers from 30+ countries. Specializes in performance, compression, swim and related fabric products including swimwear, rash guards, sports jerseys, activewear, and yoga wear. The HUGE SPORTS brand is registered in the US (USPTO) and Canada (CIPO). Holds 30 trademarks, 2 patents, and 43+ copyrights. A-level taxpayer in 2024.
